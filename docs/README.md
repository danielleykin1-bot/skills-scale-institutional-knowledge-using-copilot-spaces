## OctoAcme Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme uses a structured, phase-based approach to managing projects from idea to release. The process begins with initiation, when teams confirm the business need, identify stakeholders, define success metrics, and create a lightweight project one-pager. From there, planning turns the approved initiative into an actionable roadmap by prioritizing backlog items, identifying dependencies, estimating scope, and defining acceptance criteria and the definition of done.

During execution and tracking, the team works in an iterative rhythm with daily standups, regular progress reviews, and a project board that tracks items from backlog through QA and done. This phase emphasizes clear ownership, small increments, and visible progress so cross-functional teams can align on what is delivered and what risks still need attention. Tracking is tied to milestones, quality checks, and required delivery processes such as CI validation and review gates.

Risk management and communication are treated as continuous activities rather than one-time tasks. Team leads maintain a risk register, monitor dependencies, and escalate issues through clearly defined pathways when blockers affect delivery. Communication is frequent and role-aware, with status updates for stakeholders, weekly PM/product alignment, and structured templates for both routine reporting and incident response. This helps ensure that stakeholders have a consistent source of truth and that significant changes are identified quickly.

Quality and release readiness are built into the lifecycle as well. Teams are expected to write tests, validate critical flows, run security checks, and complete manual QA where needed before production release. Release and deployment guides standardize rollout steps, smoke testing, post-deployment verification, and rollback planning. After each milestone or release, OctoAcme emphasizes retrospectives and continuous improvement so the team can capture lessons learned, assign action items, and improve the operating model over time.

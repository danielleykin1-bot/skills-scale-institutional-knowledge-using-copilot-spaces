# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Manager / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Managers or Sponsors provide executive oversight, align the project with strategic priorities, and remove organizational barriers that affect delivery. They ensure the project remains relevant to business goals and has the support needed to succeed.

### Responsibilities
- Sponsor the initiative and confirm business value and strategic alignment
- Approve scope, funding, and priority trade-offs when needed
- Help resolve cross-functional conflicts and executive escalations
- Review major milestones, risks, and outcomes with project leadership
- Champion stakeholder engagement and communication across the organization

### Goals
- Maintain executive alignment and sponsorship across the project lifecycle
- Protect the investment and ensure expected business outcomes are achieved
- Support decision-making with clear priorities and accountability

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates and executive briefings
- Milestone reviews and business case check-ins
- Decisions on priority, funding, and stakeholder commitments

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works closely with the Project Manager to confirm milestones, priorities, and risk escalation paths.
- Provides direction to the Product Manager on business goals and trade-offs that affect roadmap and outcomes.
- Supports Developers and Technical Leads by ensuring resources, approvals, and strategic alignment are available when needed.

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide the technical direction of the solution, define design standards, and reduce architectural risk. They provide technical leadership while ensuring delivery teams remain aligned with long-term system goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture patterns, technical standards, and system constraints
- Review technical feasibility and help estimate complexity and dependencies
- Guide design decisions and support implementation quality across teams
- Identify technical risks, alternatives, and mitigation strategies
- Coordinate with engineering leaders to align delivery with platform architecture

### Goals
- Maintain a sustainable and scalable technical approach
- Reduce design risk and rework through early technical alignment
- Support faster, more reliable delivery across engineering teams

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture decision records
- Engineering planning and dependency discussions
- Technical risk and mitigation updates with project leadership

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers to review design decisions and implementation trade-offs.
- Works with Product Managers to understand customer and business constraints that affect technical feasibility.
- Collaborates with Project Managers on dependencies, sequencing, and risk planning across delivery milestones.

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads establish quality standards, validation strategies, and testing expectations across the project. They ensure that work meets defined acceptance criteria and operational readiness requirements before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy, quality gates, and acceptance criteria alignment
- Review release readiness, defect risk, and validation coverage
- Coordinate test planning, automation discussions, and issue triage
- Partner with product and engineering teams on quality expectations
- Surface risks related to product quality, user experience, and regression

### Goals
- Improve confidence in delivery quality and user experience
- Reduce critical defects and regression risk before release
- Maintain clear, measurable quality standards across the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage and quality dashboards
- Validation updates for stakeholders and engineering leads

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to review test coverage, defect triage, and release criteria.
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and user expectations.
- Supports Project Managers by communicating quality risks that may affect schedule, launch readiness, or scope decisions.

---

## Change Management Officer

### Role Summary
Change Management Officers help teams and stakeholders adapt to new processes, tools, and business changes introduced by projects. They focus on adoption, communication, and reducing resistance during transitions.

### Responsibilities
- Assess the impact of proposed changes on teams, users, and stakeholders
- Develop communication plans and training support for transitions
- Coordinate stakeholder readiness and adoption activities
- Identify barriers to adoption and recommend mitigation actions
- Measure change readiness and adoption progress after rollout

### Goals
- Reduce disruption caused by organizational or process change
- Improve stakeholder readiness and confidence in new ways of working
- Increase successful adoption of project outcomes and process improvements

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder readiness updates and adoption tracking
- Change impact briefings and training communications
- Support plans for launch, rollouts, and adoption follow-up

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers to ensure communication plans align with rollout timing and stakeholder messaging.
- Works with Product Managers to understand user or business impact and adoption success metrics.
- Supports Developers and Technical Leads by helping teams adjust to new workflows, tools, and operational practices.

---

## Resource Manager

### Role Summary
Resource Managers plan capacity, allocate talent, and help teams manage workload across multiple initiatives. They ensure critical work is staffed appropriately and that dependencies are visible before delivery commitments are made.

### Responsibilities
- Forecast capacity and identify staffing gaps across projects
- Allocate people to priorities and coordinate resource trade-offs
- Monitor workload, burnout, and team balancing across workstreams
- Support staffing decisions with project timing and scope constraints
- Coordinate with leadership on role availability and hiring or assignment needs

### Goals
- Improve team capacity planning and delivery predictability
- Reduce bottlenecks and underutilization of critical talent
- Ensure project teams have the right mix of skills at the right time

### Typical Communication
- Resource planning and staffing reviews
- Capacity dashboards and workload forecasting updates
- Escalation discussions for competing priorities and staffing gaps

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to align staffing plans with project timelines and milestone dependencies.
- Supports Product Managers and Sponsors by clarifying trade-offs between delivery priorities and team capacity.
- Coordinates with Technical Leads to ensure the appropriate expertise is available for complex technical work.

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers help ensure the project meets required controls, policies, and risk standards. They guide teams in security, privacy, and compliance decisions that affect the solution or operating model.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and review security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Assess risk and ensure required controls are implemented before delivery
- Review architecture, deployment plans, and operational safeguards
- Coordinate security-related issues, remediation planning, and approvals
- Support auditing and documentation for governance requirements

### Goals
- Reduce risk exposure and improve confidence in secure delivery
- Ensure the project meets policy, legal, and operational obligations
- Promote secure-by-default practices across teams and systems

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and control assessments
- Compliance checkpoints and remediation tracking
- Risk and governance updates with leadership stakeholders

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Advises Technical Leads and Developers on secure design standards, controls, and implementation requirements.
- Provides input to Project Managers and Sponsors on risk, remediation timing, and compliance impacts to milestones.
- Works with Product Managers to balance business needs against security and regulatory constraints.
